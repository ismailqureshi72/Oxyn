## How to read the tables
- **States**: the literal values of status/state/kind/risk/mode-style props. Where a prop has none, a `~` list is inferred from the item's description, motion and a11y text.
- **Controls**: every public prop (`=default`, `[enum]`), then `events:` callbacks, then `keys:` from the Keyboard section. `className`/spread props omitted.
- **Micro-interactions & timings**: the Motion section (reduced-motion line folded into a `[reduced-motion handled]` flag); `⏱` lists every explicit duration found in motion text or prop docs. Most items state behaviour, not numbers – the absence of ⏱ means the site gives no number.
- **Oxyn spec**: existing spec(s) that cover the item, or **NEW**; "NEW (extends x)" means no spec covers it but it would be built on x.
- **Oxyn plus**: 1–3 concrete extra variants/controls beyond affectUI.
