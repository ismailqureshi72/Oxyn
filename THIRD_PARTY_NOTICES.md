## Brand and model marks

The marks in `crates/oxyn-core/src/brand.rs` are inlined SVG paths, generated by
`scripts/brand/gen_brand.py`. They are drawn in their official colours by default (the multi-colour
`-color` file where Lobe Icons has one, else the brand colour; black-or-white brands in solid black or
white) by `oxyn_dioxus::BrandIcon` / `MarkFor` and `oxyn_gpui::brand_mark` / `brand_icon`. All trademarks and logos belong to their
owners; their use identifies the product or integration and implies no endorsement. Follow each
owner's brand guidelines when you ship them.
